Our Story

Faith, culture & connection — brewed since 2018.

He-Brews opened its doors with a simple vision: create a space in Lubbock where the community can enjoy great coffee and experience God's love.

We're Christian-owned, Hispanic-owned, and proudly student-run — a safe, comfortable place to study, read, grow relationships, or simply enjoy quiet time with a cup of excellent craft coffee.

📻

Walk by Faith Radio — live from inside the café

Lubbock's only local contemporary Christian station broadcasts right here on 98.7 FM & 1420 AM (and worldwide at 987radio.com). You might catch the hosts on-air while you sip.

Our Mission
"To Inspire Growth."

We live it every day — growing in our craft, our business, and ourselves, and making room for our customers to grow too.
